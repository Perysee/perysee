# Trademark policy

The code is open source. The name is not.

"Perysee" and the Perysee logo are trademarks of Matteo Ribardière EI. The AGPL
licenses the code, not the brand (AGPL section 7, paragraph e). This policy
says how you can use the name.

## You can

- Say that your product, module or service **works with Perysee**, **is built
  for Perysee**, or **is a module for Perysee**.
- Distribute **unmodified** Perysee releases under the Perysee name.
- Offer **unmodified** Perysee as a one-click install on your hosting platform,
  and name it Perysee.
- Write articles, tutorials and videos about Perysee and use the logo to
  illustrate them.

## You cannot, without written permission

- Use "Perysee" in the name of a **modified version** or a fork. Pick another
  name and remove the logo.
- Use "Perysee" or a confusingly similar name in a company name, product name,
  module name or domain name.
- Display the **"Official"** or **"Verified by Perysee"** badges, or imply that
  Perysee endorses your product, unless Perysee granted that status on the
  marketplace.
- Sell or white-label Perysee under its name as part of a hosting offer beyond
  a plain installer. That requires a reseller agreement.

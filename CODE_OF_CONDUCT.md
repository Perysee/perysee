# Code of conduct

Perysee is built in the open. Keep it a place where people want to contribute.

## Expected

- Be respectful, including when you disagree.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Many contributors write in a second language.
- Help newcomers.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment, discriminatory remarks or jokes.
- Personal attacks and repeated off-topic provocation.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Sexual content or unwanted advances.
- Spam and self-promotion unrelated to the discussion.

## Scope

This applies in every Perysee space: repositories, issues, pull requests,
discussions, the marketplace and social accounts of the project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to [conduct@perysee.com](mailto:conduct@perysee.com).
Reports stay confidential.

Possible responses, depending on severity: a private warning, removal of
content, a temporary ban, a permanent ban. Maintainers decide and explain their
decision to the people involved.
